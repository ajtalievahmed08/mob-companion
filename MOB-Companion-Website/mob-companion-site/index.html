01 / THE IDEA

Not another stats panel.
A teammate with a character.

MOB lives on FACEIT pages as a small movable mascot. It can react to where you are, speak up at the right moment, and open a dedicated panel when you need more detail.

◉

A companion on the page

A compact floating character that stays out of the way, can be moved around, and has its own visual personality.

03 / ROADMAP

iterations.
